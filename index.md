## GreenMiles
Use the code **200BONUS** when you sign up for GreenMiles and receive 200 bonus points - equivalent to $20 in reward value.

1. **What are GreenMiles Points?**

   Green Points are Pion Power's way of saying thank you to their EV charger users for choosing clean energy. Every time you plug in, you’re using cleaner fuel, helping cut carbon emissions, and making a positive impact on the planet.

2. **What can GreenMiles Points do?**

   Your GreenMiles Points add up to real rewards. They reflect your contribution to a cleaner future and can be redeemed for cash rewards as Pion Power's way of appreciating your efforts.

3. **How do rewards work?**

   Every 1000 kWh charged = 1000 Green Points; every 1000 Points = $100 reward. Rewards are sent to you by cheque at the end of the calendar year.

   For full details on the program, see [Green Miles Rewards](https://www.pionpowertech.com/green-miles).
