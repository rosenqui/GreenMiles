Use the code **200BONUS** when you sign up for GreenMiles and we'll both receive 200 bonus points. Equivalent to $20 in reward value.

1. **What are GreenMiles Points?**

   Green Points are a way of saying thank you to Pion Power EV charger users for choosing clean energy. Every time you plug in, you’re using cleaner fuel, helping cut carbon emissions, and making a positive impact on the planet.

2. **What can GreenMiles Points do?**

   Your Green Points add up to real rewards. They reflect your contribution to a cleaner future and can be redeemed for cash rewards as our way of appreciating your efforts.

3. **How do rewards work?**

   Every 1000 kWh charged = 1000 Green Points. Every 1000 Points = $100 reward Rewards are sent to you by cheque.
